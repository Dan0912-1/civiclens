Real bills moving through Congress and your state legislature, explained in plain language your students can finish in minutes.

CapitolKey is a strictly nonpartisan civic education tool for high school classrooms. It is free for teachers and students, and students join your class with a code in about thirty seconds. No accounts, no installs.

Why it works in class
01

Strictly nonpartisan

Explains what a bill does and who it affects. It never argues for or against a bill, party, or position. Safe to assign in any classroom.

02

Plain language, real bills

Current legislation rewritten at a high-school reading level, with a clear "if it passes" and "if it fails" breakdown.

03

No student accounts

Students join with a class code. No emails, no passwords, no app to install. Runs in any browser or on a Chromebook.

04

Built-in engagement tracking

Assign a bill with a due date, see who finished and how long they spent, and export results to CSV for your gradebook.
